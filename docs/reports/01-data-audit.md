# Recorded-sales data audit

Generated by `python scripts/audit.py`; descriptive audit, no trained model or forecast evaluation.

- Verified all 12 CSV checksums, byte sizes, schemas and row counts: **1,067,371 raw rows**.
- Coverage: 2009-12-01 07:45:00 to 2011-12-09 12:50:00; complete calendar weeks retained: 2009-12-07 through 2011-12-04.
- Missing customer IDs: 243,007; missing descriptions: 4,382. Neither field is needed for aggregate sales; missing customer IDs are retained.

## Sheet overlap and repeated rows

The sheets share 8 calendar dates in December 2010. Use the later sheet on shared dates: remove 22,523 earlier-sheet rows; 22,523 have an exact match in the retained sheet. This calendar ownership rule preserves later-sheet line multiplicity and prevents workbook overlap from doubling sales.

There are 34,335 exact repeated raw rows beyond first. After sheet reconciliation, 11,812 remain. Retain these within-sheet repeats: no line-item key establishes that they are errors. Dropping exact repeats from eligible UK sales would remove 32,224 units across complete weeks. This is a descriptive sensitivity, not a preferred correction; compare policies both ways before promotion.

## Target construction

After reconciliation: 1,044,848 rows, 19,165 cancellation-invoice rows, 22,557 nonpositive quantities and 6,029 nonpositive prices. These counts overlap and are not an additive exclusion waterfall. A separate exclusion table in JSON records nonmatching stock codes, including postage and adjustments.

Keep UK rows with positive quantity, positive price, noncancellation invoice and stock code matching the configured five-digit product pattern. The pattern is a scope heuristic, not a verified product catalog. Exclude boundary calendar weeks. This leaves 905,162 lines and 8,850,606 units. Do not net cancellations into positive sales or assume they occurred in the original sale week. Extreme positive orders are retained for later sensitivity analysis.

Choose the top 50 products by **training-only** units after requiring 26 history weeks and 13 active training weeks. They cover 21.93% of filtered training units. Do not generalize to new products or the catalog. The resulting 5,097 product-weeks have 6.83% zeros. Zero-fill only from each product's first observed complete-week sale; earlier weeks do not exist in the panel. Zeros mean no retained recorded sale, not verified in-stock zero demand. Closure, delisting and stockout status are unknown, including internal dates without transactions.

## Temporal evaluation readiness

Panel row counts: {'train': 3797, 'development': 650, 'test': 650}. Four-week rolling origins: {'development': 10, 'test': 10}. Training ends before 2011-06-06; development target weeks precede 2011-09-05; final target weeks start on that date. Target windows never cross partitions. Overlapping origins require origin-aware uncertainty, not an independent product-row test. No candidate fitting, interval calibration or final-test prediction has run.

Daily transaction timestamps are treated as local recorded wall time; no timezone is supplied. Country and SKU define the series; invoice/customer/description and contemporaneous target-week price are not model features. No inventory, supplier lead time, purchase cost, stock availability, lost-sales label or replenishment action is present. Forecast **recorded positive unit sales**, then evaluate hypothetical inventory costs with stated assumptions. See [charter](../project-charter.md) and [evaluation plan](../evaluation-plan.md).
