## Metrics, uncertainty and release gates

Primary forecast metric: equally weighted mean product-origin MASE for the four weekly predictions, using each product's **initial training-only** mean absolute one-week sales difference as denominator. If that scale is zero, report the series separately with MAE and exclude it from MASE with an explicit count. Also report unit-weighted WAPE with its denominator, weekly horizon MAE, cumulative-sales MAE, pinball loss at the specified quantiles, interval coverage and width. No MAPE on zero sales. Report by product and horizon, including low-volume, zero-heavy and extreme-order failures.

Primary inventory scenario: four-week lead/protection window, initial inventory position zero, Cu=4 and Co=1 in arbitrary cost units. Simulated realized cost is `Cu * max(actual_sum - available, 0) + Co * max(available - actual_sum, 0)`, where available equals initial inventory position plus the hypothetical order. Report cost per product-origin on the same sales targets. Sensitivity scenarios use Cu/Co ratios 1, 4 and 9 and initial inventory of zero or twice the pre-origin trailing weekly mean. These are independent single-window scenarios, not a continuous operational inventory trajectory. Lead times other than four weeks require their own versioned target horizon and completed-window calibration before being exposed in the application.

Development promotion gates: finite nonnegative outputs, lineage and leakage checks pass; mean MASE improves at least 5% over seasonal naive; primary mean scenario cost does not worsen; empirical 80% cumulative coverage is at least 75%; and no top-ten-training-volume product's MAE degrades more than 25% without documented review. Thresholds are engineering choices, not measured business benefits. Pick lowest MASE among passing candidates; fewer leaves breaks ties. If none passes, retain the baseline and publish the fitted comparison.

Final release acceptance applies the same thresholds to final results without selecting another candidate. Uncertainty: paired moving-block bootstrap over ordered origins (block length four origins, 500 replicates, seed 20261021), carrying all products together to preserve shared calendar shocks; report paired metric/cost differences and intervals. Only ten origins per partition means very limited independent temporal information, so show all origin-level results and avoid a definitive savings claim. Blocks and pooled residuals do not remove source censoring or nonstationarity.
